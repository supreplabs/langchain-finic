# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people of all backgrounds and levels
of experience. Focus feedback on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
personal attacks, and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and restrict participation
when needed. Enforcement should be proportionate and explained to the affected person
when possible. Apply these expectations consistently in issues, reviews, discussions,
and other project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the Suprep Labs organization maintainers through
an available private contact on their GitHub profiles. Do not post private details in
public issues. Security vulnerabilities use the separate reporting path in SECURITY.md.
